OUR STORY

Connecting Farms With Families

Sage & Soil was created with one simple vision— to make fresh, locally grown produce accessible while empowering Indian farmers through fair and sustainable trade.

Our Story

Sage & Soil began with a simple belief that everyone deserves access to fresh, nutritious food while supporting the farmers who grow it.

We connect local farms with families across India, creating a transparent supply chain that benefits both communities and the environment.

🌱 Our Promise

Fresh from farm to doorstep, with honesty, sustainability, and care in every delivery.

OUR PURPOSE

Guided by Purpose

Every decision we make is rooted in sustainability, transparency, and supporting local farming communities.

Our Mission

To connect every household with fresh, locally grown produce while ensuring fair opportunities and better livelihoods for farmers across India.

Our Vision

To build a future where sustainable farming, conscious consumption, and healthy communities thrive together through technology and trust.

OUR JOURNEY

From One Farm to Hundreds

Every milestone reflects our commitment to empowering farmers and delivering fresher food.

2022

The Beginning

Sage & Soil started with just five local farmers in Kerala, driven by the dream of making fresh produce accessible to everyone.

2023

Growing the Network

Expanded operations across South India and partnered with over 50 sustainable farms.

2024

Subscription Launch

Introduced flexible weekly produce subscriptions for families and working professionals.

2025

Growing Nationwide

Reached hundreds of partner farms and thousands of happy households across India.
